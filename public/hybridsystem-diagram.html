HYBRIDSYSTEM operating diagram

The app centers on a controlled mission loop: playbooks create work, agents execute steps, humans approve higher authority actions, and memory feeds the next run.

Primary Loop Launch mission, process steps, review outputs, then store approved lessons.
Human Override Higher authority tools stop at explicit approve, revise, or reject decisions.
Demo State Seed data and browser storage make the prototype feel live during refreshes.
Blueprint The app includes the product spec, stack map, RLS schema, and cost model.
